## What We Do

At DegenLlama, we develop **autonomous agents** and **web3 solutions** to advance automation and uphold human rights in the AI-driven world. Our mission is to create intelligent systems that operate independently, integrate seamlessly with decentralized networks, automate complex tasks, and protect individual freedoms against emerging threats. We're also fostering a **DegenLlama DAO**, where the community co-creates the future of ethical tech.

- **Autonomous Agents**: Build and deploy AI that thinks, acts, and evolves on its own, tailored for real-world impact.
- **Web3 Integration**: Leverage blockchain for secure, transparent automation and ownership in decentralized ecosystems.
- **Automation Tools**: Simplify workflows, boost efficiency, and free up time for what matters—while prioritizing privacy and rights.
- **Human Rights Focus**: Advocate for ethical AI, data sovereignty, and equitable access in the new digital frontier.
